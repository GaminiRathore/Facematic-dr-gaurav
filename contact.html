What happens at a first consultation?

A full examination — dental, facial or both — followed by an honest conversation about what is realistic. You leave with a written plan covering sequence, number of sessions and cost. There is no obligation to start the same day.

Do you treat teeth and skin in the same visit?

Often, yes. That is the point of the clinic. Where treatments need to be spaced — for example injectables around dental work — we will sequence them properly and explain why.

How much does treatment cost?

It depends entirely on what you need, which is why we do not publish a price list. What we do promise is that the full cost is written down before anything begins, and it does not change mid-course.

Are fillers and Botox safe?

In trained hands, with genuine products and correct dosing, they have a long safety record. Dr. Bagaria will go through the risks specific to your case at the consultation, and will decline to treat if something in your history makes it unwise.

How long before I see results from hair therapy?

Regenerative therapies such as PRP, GFC and exosomes work over a course, not a session. Most patients are reviewed with photographs at three months. We will tell you at that point whether continuing is worth your money.

Is there parking?

Street parking is available along Sarat Bose Road. If you are driving in at a busy hour, call ahead and the front desk will tell you where there is space.
