Technology

The equipment behind the results.

Platforms are chosen for what they do well on Indian skin — not for what looks impressive in a brochure.

HydraFacial

Cosderma 10-in-1
Deep cleansing · Hydration

A 10-in-1 hydradermabrasion system. Oxygen-bubble cleansing and gentle suction clear the pores, then ultrasound, bipolar RF and hot–cold handpieces hydrate, firm and calm the skin in one session.

Dermapen

Pro microneedling
Scars · Pores · Texture

An automated microneedling pen that makes thousands of fine, vertical micro-channels at a set depth. The skin’s own repair response then builds new collagen and elastin, softening scars and pores.

Nanopore EMSES RF

by Mediderma
Firming · Fine lines

Radiofrequency, microcurrent (EMS) and blue LED light in one handpiece. Used to firm loose skin and soften expression lines, with a refreshed look straight after the session.

The full equipment list is gone through at your consultation, along with what each one is — and isn't — suitable for.
