Dental & Facial Aesthetics

Facematic began with a frustration. A patient would come in for a smile makeover, get a beautiful set of teeth — and still not look the way they had hoped, because nobody had looked at the face around them.

Dr. Bagaria built this clinic to fix that. He plans dentistry and facial aesthetics as a single design problem: proportion, profile, skin quality and smile, considered in the same appointment. It is an approach that has earned him prestigious awards and recognitions, and it is the standard he has built the rest of the team around.

He has worked with and learned from colleagues in Dubai, Italy and Germany, and brings that thinking home to Kolkata — alongside a quieter conviction: that the best aesthetic work is the kind nobody can point to. He is also a member of the World Human Rights Protection Commission, which falls under the Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi.

How we practise

Four things we
will not compromise.

Aesthetic medicine has a reputation problem in this country. These are the rules we run the clinic by.

I.

Diagnose first

No treatment is quoted before it is examined. If the cause is medical, we treat the cause.

II.

One plan, one price

You leave the first appointment with the full sequence and the full cost in writing.

III.

Nothing overdone

We work in small increments and review. It is always easier to add than to undo.

IV.

Say no when needed

If a treatment will not give you what you are hoping for, we tell you before you pay for it.
