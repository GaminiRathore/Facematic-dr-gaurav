How a course runs

Four steps, and no surprises in between.

Step 01

Consult

A full examination and an honest conversation about what is realistic. Forty minutes, unhurried.

Step 02

Plan

A written plan with sequence, sessions and cost. You take it home before you decide anything.

Step 03

Treat

Sessions scheduled around your calendar, with photographs recorded at each stage.

Step 04

Review

We measure the result against the plan — and tell you when you are finished.

Not sure which of these you need?

That is exactly what the first appointment is for. Come in, and we will tell you what we would do — including nothing, if that is the right answer.
